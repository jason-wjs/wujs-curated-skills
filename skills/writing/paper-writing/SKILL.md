# Paper Writing

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

Keep claims tied to supplied evidence, including comparator, regime, assumptions,
uncertainty, and the distinction between results and proposed explanations.
A local edit needs only relevant context. A new paper or major restructure may
benefit from a claim-evidence map and a paper plan; do not require these for
sentence polishing.

## Choose relevant resources

- For theory-led work, use the [theory outline](assets/theory-paper-outline.md).
- For empirical methods or mechanism studies, use the
  [empirical outline](assets/empirical-paper-outline.md).
- For three paper archetypes illustrated by Action Chunking, DPPO, OGPO, and
  Much Ado About Noising, consult [paper conventions](references/group-paper-conventions.md).
  These are reference authors' choices, not mandatory structures or a claim
  that their voice is the user's own.
- For a new argument, use [paper-plan.md](assets/paper-plan.md) when useful.
- For figure narrative, use [figure-storyboard.md](assets/figure-storyboard.md).
- For research sentence examples, consult [sentence-style.md](references/sentence-style.md).
- For a LaTeX task, use [project conventions](references/latex-project-conventions.md).
  The [skeleton](assets/latex-paper-skeleton/paper-main.tex) is for new projects;
  preserve an existing project's structure and venue requirements.

## Draft and revise

Organize around the scientific question and evidence. Distinguish a new method,
a theoretical result, and an explanation of existing performance. Show why the
contribution follows from the evidence; avoid an inventory of equal-weight
claims. When the body changes materially, check that the title and abstract
still represent it. For abstract-specific work, use
[abstract-writing](../abstract-writing/SKILL.md) without running another prose
pipeline afterward.

Bundled analyses work without the original papers. Inspect original examples
only when needed for the task, using a supplied corpus directory. Ask for a
missing corpus only when matching those examples depends on it. Keep private
papers outside this collection and distinguish inspected evidence from bundled
analysis. Do not claim to have read original papers from these notes alone.

Return the requested text and any consequential evidence gaps. Human guides
live in `for-humans/writing/` in a wujs-curated-skills checkout; they are not
installed with skills. Consult them only for an explicit guide or template task.
