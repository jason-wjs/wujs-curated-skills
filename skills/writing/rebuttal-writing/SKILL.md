# Rebuttal Writing

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

For a complete response, map the supplied reviews to answers using the paper,
decision letter, venue rules, and available evidence. The
[worksheet](assets/rebuttal-worksheet.md) can help with many concerns. A local
prose edit does not require a full response strategy.

Answer the technical concern early. Explain agreement, disagreement, correction,
or missing evidence in calm language. Avoid ritual praise and blaming the
reviewer. A brief courtesy is appropriate when requested or conventional for
the venue; do not enforce a fixed sentence count.

Keep reviewer quotations, author replies, and proposed manuscript edits distinct.
Name concrete revisions and distinguish completed experiments, existing paper
results, future edits, and untested cases. Never write that an experiment or
revision is complete without evidence.

Bound concessions and claims precisely. We do not claim X; we establish Y can
be the clearest answer here. Do not run an anti-defensive deletion pass.

- For evidence planning, see [evidence and risk](references/evidence-and-risk.md).
- For specific concern types, see [response patterns](references/response-patterns.md).
- For author-team language examples, see [response examples](references/response-examples.md).

Return the response and unresolved author decisions; preserve the venue limit.
