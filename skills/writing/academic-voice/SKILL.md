# Academic Voice

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

Develop the argument for an intelligent reader. Explain enough for the next
inference, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and give important
intellectual distinctions appropriate space. Keep first-person judgment and
purposeful variation when they belong to the author.

Prefer connected explanation to slogans, changelog bullets, manufactured
hooks, or motivational endings. Preserve formal terms when they belong to the
actual method; replace abstract nouns and managerial metaphors when they hide
the actor or relation. Academic voice need not be impersonal or compressed.

Match the genre: exposition explains, while a manual may instruct. Do not turn
an analysis into repeated should/must advice. Return the revised passage or
requested voice findings without invoking other editing skills.
