# General Writing

Apply [editing scope and voice](references/editing-contract.md). Match the
requested sentence or paragraph scope before editing. Use the supplied draft
and author samples as voice evidence; preserve purposeful irregularity.

Improve the actual relation between ideas, concrete subjects and verbs, and
reader comprehension. Remove filler without replacing precise detail with
generic polished prose. Do not infer AI authorship from stylistic patterns.

For a difficult revision or requested audit, use the short
[evaluation guide](references/eval.md). A specific problem may warrant the
sibling semantic-usage, cadence, or voice reference; none is a mandatory pass.
Return one edited version unless alternatives are useful or requested.
