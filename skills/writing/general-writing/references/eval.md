# Revision review

Use this guide for a requested audit or a difficult edit; it does not prescribe
another rewrite cycle.

- Does the revision honor sentence versus paragraph scope?
- Are facts, arguments, evidence verbs, necessary conditions, and author voice intact?
- Are inferred logical links supported, with new premises or evidence gaps outside the prose?
- Does the reader understand the actual relation between ideas?
- Have redundant defenses, empty importance claims, and formulaic endings been reduced?
- Do rhythm, lists, contrasts, and punctuation serve this genre and argument?
- For a full draft, do the title, abstract, headings, and transitions still fit the body?

Leave clear existing language alone. Findings should identify a concrete issue
and useful correction, not assign an AI-authorship score.
