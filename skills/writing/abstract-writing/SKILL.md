# Abstract Writing

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

Base the abstract on the supplied manuscript or notes. Read the central result
and its conditions when available; distinguish planned work from completed
work. Clarify missing results if otherwise the abstract would invent evidence.

Make the problem, central answer, decisive support, and demonstrated scope
intelligible without the paper. Use the order and number of sentences that
serve that argument. Prefer the decisive comparison to a catalog of sections,
benchmarks, or contributions. Explain the conceptual move before listing
components whose purpose would be unclear.

Keep necessary qualifications adjacent to the claims they constrain. Match
verbs to evidence; correlation does not establish a mechanism. End where the
argument reaches a supported consequence, boundary, or unresolved question.
Do not append generic importance or repeat the previous sentence.

For concrete models, consult [example abstract patterns](references/example-abstract-patterns.md).
They analyze four reference papers, not a fixed template. After a body revision,
check the abstract against the final results and scope. No mandatory Humanizer
or anti-defensive pass follows.
