# Anti-Defensive Writing

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

State what the text argues, examines, or establishes. Remove a disclaimer only
when it adds no useful scope, evidence, conceptual distinction, or reader
understanding. Keep genuine negative findings and methodological limitations.

Replace stacked empty hedges with the evidential verb the source supports.
Never turn suggests into demonstrates, add a performance number, or invent a
positive scope to replace a limitation. Necessary qualifications may remain
in an abstract or conclusion even when stated elsewhere.

Consult [rewrite examples](references/rewrite-examples.md) when a concrete
comparison helps. For reviewer responses, use
[rebuttal-writing](../rebuttal-writing/SKILL.md): a direct statement of what is
not claimed can be essential to answering the reviewer.
