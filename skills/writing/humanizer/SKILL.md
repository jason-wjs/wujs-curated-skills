# Humanizer

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

Treat stylistic patterns as evidence of an editing opportunity, not evidence
of AI authorship. Improve the passage for its reader and genre. Do not invent
personal reactions, opinions, facts, or a more casual voice to make it human.

Consult [pattern examples](references/patterns.md) only for relevant problems.
Preserve technical terms, meaningful repetition, lists that form real sets,
and purposeful punctuation. No word blacklist or list-count quota applies.

Return one final revision, or findings when diagnosis was requested. Do not
produce a mandatory draft/audit/final sequence or invoke sibling style passes.
For an existing file, follow the user's requested edit or review mode.
