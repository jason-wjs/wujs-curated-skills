# Grant Writing

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

Use the call, scoring criteria, questions, format, and length limits as the
application contract. Establish why the problem matters, the proposed research,
why the team can execute it, and the value to the sponsor.

Read [sponsor types](references/sponsor-types.md) when shaping fit. For examples
from Amazon, BNY, and Jane Street proposals, consult
[proposal patterns](references/example-proposal-patterns.md). These analyses
are bundled; original private proposals are not. Use a supplied example corpus
when needed and never infer results or commitments from another proposal.

Distinguish preliminary results from aims, hypotheses, targets, and planned
work. Remove empty aspiration without converting a goal into an achieved
result. Tie milestones to scientific decisions and resources to actual scope.
Flag missing facts or applicant commitments outside polished text, or use
clearly marked placeholders when an incomplete draft is requested.

For unresolved story choices, use [grant-planning](../grant-planning/SKILL.md).
Do not force a planning stage for a local revision. Return the requested
application text and unresolved decisions, with no mandatory style-pass chain.
