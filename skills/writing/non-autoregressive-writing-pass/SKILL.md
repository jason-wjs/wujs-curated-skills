# Whole-Draft Openings and Transitions

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

The retained command name denotes revision with knowledge of the whole draft;
it does not describe a model architecture or a required drafting method.

For a full-draft review, read the title, headings, and paragraph openings as an
argument spine. Check that they frame the actual material and do not preserve
an earlier emphasis. Then inspect each paragraph ending beside the next opening:
identify continuation, contrast, cause, qualification, or change of question.

Repair stale headings, unclear referents, redundant restarts, and transitions
that imply unsupported causal relations. An opening may set necessary context
before the claim; do not force every claim into the first sentence. Endings
should complete their own reasoning without becoming trailers for the next
section. Adjacency and repeated technical terms may supply enough continuity.

Read the revised passage in context and preserve deliberate breaks. For a
local request, inspect neighboring text without expanding the editing scope.
