# Writing Cadence

Apply the [shared editing defaults](../general-writing/references/editing-contract.md) to the requested passage.

Read rhythm at paragraph scale. Repetition, short runs, and uniform lengths are
diagnostics rather than failures. Technical taxonomies and deliberate emphasis
may need repeated syntax. Sentence-level requests still default to light edits.

Let sentence length follow logical relations: combine a cause with its result
when that helps, or separate independent inferences. Link familiar information
to new claims without mechanically varying sentence lengths or swapping terms.
Keep meaningful contrasts while removing repetitive not-X-but-Y framing.

Let paragraphs end when their reasoning finishes. Do not append a slogan or
mini-summary. Use [cadence principles](references/cadence-principles.md) for a
full cadence review. Preserve facts, qualifications, and purposeful abruptness.
