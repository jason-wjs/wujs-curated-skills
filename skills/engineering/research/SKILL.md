# Research

Investigate the question against primary sources: official documentation,
source code, specifications, and first-party APIs. Distinguish source facts,
inferences, and unresolved questions. Match the depth to the decision at hand.

Save a concise cited Markdown note at the requested location or the project's
existing research-note location. Include the answer, supporting evidence, and
limitations; link sources next to the claims they support.

Research directly unless a background agent is available, authorized, and
useful alongside independent work. Delegation is optional, not a prerequisite.
